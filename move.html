Give through GateCity Buckhead
  1. Andrew Faletti Ministry Support is already chosen for you.
  2. Enter your amount. If you give monthly now, use the same amount.
  3. For a monthly gift, tap Monthly. It will say "Monthly on the 1st starting 12/1/2026." Tap the pencil next to it and change the start date to January 1, 2027.
  4. Pick bank account or card. A bank account means a smaller processing fee.
  5. Enter your email or phone and the code it sends you. That's it, and a receipt comes by email.
