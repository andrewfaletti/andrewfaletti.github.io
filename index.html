We are the Falettis. For nearly two decades we've given our lives to planting the good news of God's kingdom in and around Atlanta. We'd love to invite you along for the journey. And we've still got a few decades left in us.

Shepherding & heralding

Right now that means giving ourselves to the flourishing of GateCity Buckhead: pastoral care, preaching and teaching, discipleship, and a church culture rooted in prayer, fellowship, and worship.

Raising leaders & building teams

Building teams, training leaders, reaching people who are far from God, and calling God's people into the good work of planting the Gospel.

Gospel planting

The Gospel is the root system of the church. We're praying and preparing for God to open new opportunities to plant it among communities that haven't heard yet, and raising three kids who love Jesus along the way.

To our partner team: none of this happens without you. Thank you.
