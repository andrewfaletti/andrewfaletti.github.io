We have some fun news

Friends,

We built a website where we are collecting sermons, podcasts, and the writing I have been doing, and we are moving these updates to email so we can get them to you more often. More on that at the end.

Here is where we are.

One

I came for the training but got something unexpected

In May I finished a nine month church planting cohort with Metro Atlanta Collective (MAC), meeting weekly with other planters from around Atlanta. I signed up for the cohort to be trained, but what I got was closer to restoration. After serving in full time ministry since 2007, I had collected some wear and tear on my soul, and I did not realize how badly I needed to be around other men carrying the same weight and walking out the same mission in an open and vulnerable environment. That turned out to be its own kind of healing.

Ashley and me with our marriage retreat facilitators.

Ashley and I also got to go on a marriage retreat as part of the cohort, a good reminder that planting a church is a family calling and not a solo assignment.

I came out refired, with more vision for planting churches and multiplying disciples than I have had in years. I am already seeing the fruit of it in how I lead as executive pastor at GateCity Buckhead. One thing the cohort gave me that I have since passed on to our church community: at this stage, everyone who is with us is a church planter, not just our staff leadership team.

Planting a church in Atlanta? Jump into MAC's second Tuesday gatherings. metroatlantacollective.com

 

Two

Our girls started knocking on the doors

Over the summer our family helped lead two backpack drive outreaches through the Garden Hills neighborhood, inviting neighbors to donate school supplies for kids in our city who need them. Partway through, our girls started knocking on the doors themselves, telling strangers about our church and about the chance to bless kids who needed supplies.

44

backpacks delivered

Three

They came for the jump houses

The next day we hosted our annual Family Fun Day, where instead of our normal service we give the morning to fellowship, food, and carnival style games. A family from Garden Hills came for their first time. Another family had been meaning to visit for months and happened to pick that week. They had been hesitant because their three kids would have to adjust and make new friends, and they walked in to jump houses, games, and cotton candy, which was the perfect environment for them to make new friends right away.

They experienced more fellowship in that one morning than in the past couple of months.

This is exactly who we want to be.
