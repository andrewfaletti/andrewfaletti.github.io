Something is stirring

Friends,

Something is stirring, and we want you to hear about it while it is still early.

Last week Hazen, our senior pastor at GateCity Buckhead, and I attended a conference on human flourishing at Trilith Live. We heard from researchers, including Jon Clifton, the CEO of Gallup, whose teams have interviewed hundreds of thousands of people about what actually moves a human life from brokenness to flourishing. Their answer came down to one word.

Love.

It was fascinating to hear years of large-scale research and polling land on the very thing the Word of God has told us all along. The greatest commandment is to love God and love one another.

Inside the main hall at Trilith Live.

Breadcrumbs

The conference was only part of it

Over lunch, Hazen and I sat with seasoned leaders whose hands are in all kinds of fruitful church planting and gospel expansion work. I also connected with a local family in the Trilith area who felt like a possible long-term person of peace for that community, an answer to a prayer I have been praying for the past couple of weeks. And Hazen and I have been invited to a leadership training in Austin, Texas at the end of October, with a very generous offer to cover our flights and hotel.

Honestly, most of this still feels like breadcrumbs. They are leading somewhere exciting and mysterious, and God has not fully disclosed where. But it is clear that He is stirring something fresh in us around planting and leadership development. GateCity Buckhead was never meant to be the last church we help start, and it feels like He is setting us and our GateCity family up for future adventures.

Would you pray with us this month?

One thing: for wisdom, discernment, and resources as we follow the breadcrumbs, that we would move with God's voice and at the pace of His Spirit.
